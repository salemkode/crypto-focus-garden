Questions.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. The app creates a small wallet for you on your phone. You only need a little BCH to pay network fees, and you can link a wallet you already use to receive your trees.

What does a tree cost?

A planting pays a tiny network fee plus a 1,000-satoshi deposit. The deposit stays with the tree and comes back to you when you claim it.

Is my focus really verified?

The chain verifies time, not attention: your tree stays locked for the full session. Pair it with app blocking to keep the time honest.

What if I lose my phone?

Back up your app wallet's recovery phrase. Your trees belong to your reward wallet, and that wallet can claim them on its own, even without the app.

Does anyone run a server I depend on?

The current app needs a configured garden service for on-chain actions. Focus Forest and Focus Directory define the on-chain rules.
